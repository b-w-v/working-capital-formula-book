Working Capital – Important Formula Book

1. Basic formulas

ParticularsFormula
Annual costAnnual production × Cost per unit
Production cost/unitRM + Labour + Manufacturing overhead
Cash overhead/unitTotal overhead − Depreciation
Cash production cost/unitRM + Labour + Cash overhead
Cash costTotal cost − Non-cash expenses

2. Current Assets formulas

A. Raw Material Stock

RM = (Annual RM Cost × Holding Days) ÷ 360

B. Work-in-Progress (WIP)

First calculate:

WIP Cost/unit = RM + (Labour × Labour completion %) + (OH × OH completion %)

Then:

WIP = (Annual WIP Cost × WIP Days) ÷ 360

C. Finished Goods

FG = (Annual Production Cost × FG Days) ÷ 360

D. Debtors

Debtors = (Annual Cost × Credit Sales % × Debtor Days) ÷ 360

E. Cash

Cash = Given Cash Balance

Total Current Assets

CA = RM + WIP + FG + Debtors + Cash

3. Current Liabilities formulas

ParticularsFormula
CreditorsAnnual RM cost × Credit days ÷ 360
Outstanding wagesAnnual labour cost × Wage lag ÷ 360
Outstanding overheadsAnnual overhead cost × OH lag ÷ 360

CL = Creditors + Outstanding Wages + Outstanding OH

4. Final Working Capital formulas

NWC = Current Assets − Current Liabilities

Contingency = NWC × Contingency %

WC Required = NWC + Contingency

Gross Working Capital

GWC = Total Current Assets

Remember: Gross WC does not subtract current liabilities.

5. When the question gives months instead of days

Amount = (Annual Cost × Months) ÷ 12

Holding periodFormula multiplier
0.5 month0.5 ÷ 12
1 month1 ÷ 12
2 months2 ÷ 12
3 months3 ÷ 12

For days, use days ÷ 360 (or 365 if the question specifies 365).

6. Operating Cycle and Cash Conversion Cycle

Average balances

Average Stock = (Opening Stock + Closing Stock) ÷ 2

Average Debtors = (Opening Debtors + Closing Debtors) ÷ 2

Average Creditors = (Opening Creditors + Closing Creditors) ÷ 2

Holding periods

Inventory Period = (Average Inventory ÷ COGS) × 365

Debtors Period = (Average Debtors ÷ Sales) × 365

Creditors Period = (Average Creditors ÷ COGS) × 365

Final cycles

Operating Cycle = Inventory Period + Debtors Period

CCC = Operating Cycle − Creditors Period

7. Important exam rules — do not forget

  1. Cash-cost basis: Remove depreciation.
  2. WIP: Use 100% raw material if fully consumed; apply completion percentages to labour and overhead.
  3. Finished goods: Use full production cost, excluding non-cash depreciation when cash-cost basis is required.
  4. Debtors: Use credit sales percentage, not total sales, when only part of sales is on credit.
  5. Creditors: Usually use raw material purchases/cost, according to the question.
  6. Contingency: Add it after calculating net working capital.
